Good footage deserves a good edit

Filming is the easy part. The edit decides whether anyone watches to the end. Pacing, captions, sound and the first three seconds do most of the work.

Our editors cut for the platform the video will live on. A reel is not a shortened YouTube video, and an ad is not a reel with a logo on it. Each gets its own treatment.

Delivery is fast and predictable, so you can post on a schedule instead of waiting on a backlog.

Who this is for

  • Creators and podcasters who film more than they can edit
  • E-commerce brands running video ads that need fresh variations
  • Coaches, consultants and agencies posting on a schedule
  • Companies that need a clean corporate or explainer video
  • Marketing teams turning webinars and events into clips

What's included

Every edit includes cutting, captions where they belong, music, basic colour and export in the right sizes. Pick the kind of video you need.

Frequently asked questions

How fast do you turn videos around?

Most single videos come back in 2–4 days. Batches and longer edits are scheduled in the proposal. Rush turnarounds can be arranged in advance.

What do I need to send you?

Your raw footage, any logos or brand files, and a short brief: the platform, the length, and what the video should make people do. A shared drive link is fine.

Which formats do you deliver?

Whatever the platform needs: 9:16 for reels and shorts, 16:9 for YouTube, 1:1 or 4:5 for feeds, and MP4 masters you can keep.

Do you add captions?

Yes. Accurate captions are included on social video by default, styled to your brand. Most people watch with the sound off.

Can you edit on a recurring schedule?

Yes. Many clients send footage weekly and get a steady stream of finished videos back. We agree the volume and the cadence up front.

Have footage waiting to be edited?

Send a sample and tell us where it will be posted. We will show you what it could become.
