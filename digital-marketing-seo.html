Frequently asked questions

How long until SEO shows results?

Technical fixes and on-page work usually show movement within 60–90 days. Competitive keywords take longer. We report every month, so you always know where things stand.

Can you run ads without doing SEO, or the other way round?

Yes. Each service stands on its own. Most clients start with one and add the next when they see results, because the channels feed each other.

Which platforms do you manage?

Google Search, Display and Shopping; Facebook and Instagram; and for organic social, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn. If you use another platform, ask.

Do we keep our own accounts?

Always. Ad accounts, social profiles, analytics and search console stay in your name. We work inside them with the access you grant, and that access is returned when we finish.

Who writes the content?

Our writers do, with AI tools used for research and first drafts. A person edits every piece for accuracy and your brand voice before it goes anywhere.
