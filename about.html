Who we are

Kavyro Solutions is an outsourcing partner based in the Philippines. Businesses in the United States, Australia, the United Kingdom and elsewhere hand us the roles they do not want to hire for locally — marketing, search, websites, video, design and day-to-day admin — and we staff them with English-fluent specialists who work on that account and no one else's.

We started from a simple observation: small and growing businesses were juggling a freelancer here, an agency there, and a lot of admin in between. Nobody owned the whole picture, and hiring locally for every gap was never going to happen. Outsourcing solves it when the people are dedicated, supervised and accountable. That part is our job.

The work is on the portfolio page rather than in a number: client websites in the United States and Australia, custom systems running inside businesses and a local government office, and campaign creative published on live client accounts across healthcare, wellness, rescue and retail.

How outsourcing works

Every engagement follows the same five steps, whether you are outsourcing one assistant or a whole marketing function.

  1. 01
    Discovery callA short call about the work you want to hand over, what you have tried, and what success looks like.
  2. 02
    Role & scopeA written scope with the tasks, the hours to be covered, the deliverables and who decides what.
  3. 03
    Match & onboardWe put the specialist forward, set up access, communication channels and a shared task board, and agree your working hours. Most roles start within 3–5 business days of sign-off.
  4. 04
    They deliverYour specialist works your account in your hours, with regular updates and built-in feedback rounds.
  5. 05
    Review & scaleWe look at the output together, adjust the scope, and add roles when you are ready.

What we carry: recruitment, training, supervision, quality checks, equipment and cover when someone is sick or moves on. What stays with you: the priorities, the approvals and the accounts.

How we use AI

AI is part of how our specialists work, not a label on the door. It removes repetitive work and speeds up first drafts, and the person on your account stays responsible for every decision and every deliverable.

  • Content: research, outlines and first drafts are AI-assisted; your writer edits everything for accuracy and your voice.
  • Reporting: dashboards and reports build themselves from live data, so they arrive on time and are never copied by hand.
  • Workflows: lead follow-up, CRM updates and hand-offs run automatically where rules can do the job, so outsourced hours go to work that needs judgement.
  • Targeting: audience and campaign decisions are informed by models, then made by people.

We choose business-grade tools with proper data terms, keep your data inside your own accounts, and name every tool we use in the proposal. Read more on our AI automation page.

What we stand for

  • Dedicated, not pooled. The same person works your account every day. You are not filing tickets into a queue.
  • Output over hours. Every role has a scope and a measure attached. You review work delivered, not timesheets.
  • Transparent and flexible. No surprises, clear communication, and roles you can add, change or pause as the business moves.
  • Your ownership. Accounts, content, designs and data stay in your systems and your name. Everyone on your account signs an NDA, and access is revocable the day you ask.
