Your time should go where it earns the most

Every business owner has a list of tasks that must get done but do not need them to do it. Inbox. Scheduling. Data entry. Follow-ups. It adds up to hours every week.

A Kavyro virtual assistant takes those tasks off your plate. You get a skilled, reliable person who learns how you work, follows clear procedures, and reports back without being chased.

Because our VAs sit next to a full digital team, they can also loop in a designer, an editor or a marketer when a task needs one.

Your data and access

You decide what the VA can see. Access is granted through your own accounts, scoped to the task, and removed the moment it is no longer needed.

Our team works under written confidentiality terms, uses two-factor authentication on every account, and keeps your information inside your systems rather than copying it out.

Frequently asked questions

What tasks can a virtual assistant handle?

Anything repeatable that lives in email, a calendar, a spreadsheet, a CRM or a support inbox. If you can describe it, we can document it and hand it to a VA. Specialist work like design, video or ads goes to the relevant team.

How do I hand over work?

We start with a short list of the tasks that cost you the most time. During onboarding we write simple procedures for each one, agree how you want updates, and then take them off your plate one by one.

Is my data safe?

Access is granted by you, at the level you choose, and returned when we finish. Our team works under confidentiality terms, uses two-factor authentication, and never moves your data out of your own systems.

Part-time or full-time?

Either. Some clients need a few hours a week; others need a dedicated full-time assistant. We size the engagement to your workload and adjust as it changes.

Can a VA work alongside your marketing team?

Yes, and it is a common setup: the VA handles scheduling, inbox and CRM while our marketing team runs campaigns, so nothing falls between the two.

Ready to get your week back?

Tell us the three tasks you would hand off tomorrow. We will start there.
